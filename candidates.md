# Candidates — Deadpan Forecasts for Cities That Do Not Exist

Brief (from kit RULES.txt / competition.yaml): one single line, 1–160 characters,
UTF-8, not only whitespace, written to `entry.txt`. A weather forecast for a city
that does not exist — absurd yet believable, read like a real broadcast line.

Judge: z-ai/glm-5.3-flash on OpenRouter (house call: superbot, reasoning high),
briefed as a dry, deadpan TV meteorologist. Pairwise, no ties.

Criteria, in the brief's exact order (each scored /5, total /25):
- C1 On-subject: one-line forecast for a nonexistent city, absurd yet believable.
- C2 Funnier: joke lands on first read; deadpan twist, not random absurdity pile.
- C3 More vivid: concrete image, number, or detail that makes the place feel real.
- C4 Broadcast tone: plain, confident, believable, straight-faced read-aloud.
- C5 Economy: uses its 160 characters well; tight, well paced, no padding.
